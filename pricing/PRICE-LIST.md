## 4. The three ways to buy

**Per order.** Pay as you go, no commitment. Best for a first batch or a one-off vehicle.

**Prepaid block.** Buy a block of stickers up front at your volume price, draw them down as
stock turns over. You get the volume price, a locked-in price, and we get the cash to print
before you have sold a single car. **This is the recommended route** — one file per dealer,
one invoice, no chasing.

**Subscription.** The dashboard, the branding and the lead alerts. Add it to whichever route
you buy stickers on.

---

## 5. What we need from you

1. **Your stock** — make, model, year, price, mileage, fuel, transmission, colour.
2. **Your specifications** — body type, engine, gearbox, drive, seats, trim, condition.
   *The A4 spec card cannot be produced without them, and they are what the buyer reads.*
3. **Photos** — front, rear, both sides, interior, dashboard, engine bay.
4. **Your contact details** and WhatsApp number.
5. **Your logo** — for white-label branding.
6. **Your address** and opening hours.
